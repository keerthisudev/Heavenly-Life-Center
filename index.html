WHAT WE BELIEVE

Our Statement of Faith

The foundation of our faith and life together.

The Bible

We believe the Holy Bible is God's inspired Word and the foundation for faith and Christian living.

Jesus Christ

We believe Jesus Christ is the Son of God, our Lord and Savior, who died and rose again.

Salvation

We believe salvation is by God's grace through faith in Jesus Christ.

Holy Spirit

We believe the Holy Spirit works in believers, guiding, comforting and empowering them.

Holy Baptism & Holy Communion

“In baptism, we are united with Christ, buried with Him in His death, and raised to new life in His grace.”
“At the Lord’s Supper, we remember His sacrifice, receive His love, and are strengthened for the journey of faith.”
